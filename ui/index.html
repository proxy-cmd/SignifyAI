Gesture Guide

Hand Signals for Recognition

H2O

Index Finger Up

Triggers the intent Need Water for hydration requests.

HELP

Open Palm

Signals Need Help when assistance is required.

OK

Thumb Up

Confirms I Am OK for reassurance and status checks.

RN

Raised Hand

Activates Call Nurse for direct caregiver attention.

Recognition Notes

  • Keep the hand clearly visible inside the camera frame.
  • Hold the gesture steadily for accurate prediction.
  • Avoid very fast movement or partial hand visibility.
  • Ensure proper room lighting for cleaner frame detection.

Eye Blink Guide

Eye-Based Patient Interaction

EYE

Eye-based commands support patients who cannot comfortably perform full hand gestures.

  • Blink once slowly to send a standard blink interaction event.
  • Blink twice to trigger the emergency pathway for urgent attention.
  • Keep the face visible and centered for stable tracking.
  • Look toward the camera and screen during interaction.
  • Do not move too far away from the device during detection.
